The loss

Water leaves the dam. Far less of it arrives.

Roughly a third of treated supply is lost in transmission across Indian networks — split between cracks nobody can see and taps nobody authorised. The two look identical on a monthly bill, which is why neither gets fixed.

The grid · junction monitoring

A meter every five kilometres turns a missing volume into a zone.

Each junction carries a flow meter, a pressure transducer, a DHT11 for chamber humidity and an acoustic sensor clamped to the pipe curve, where turbulence makes a leak loudest. Pick a junction to read it.

Core innovation · the Smart Ball

It cannot see a satellite, so it counts its own turns instead.

Dropped in at the flagged junction, the ball travels with the water and writes its own map. Drag to rotate it. Open the shell to see what is inside.

  • ESP8266 NodeMCU

    The central processing unit that orchestrates the sensors, processes telemetry data in real-time, and handles wireless transmission when the ball is retrieved.

  • Inertial mapping, not satellites

    The IMU integrates every bend, dip and rise the ball takes. Matched against the as-built drawing, that trace places the ball to within a metre of pipe — and any branch that is not on the drawing is an illegal tap-off.

  • Listening for the hiss

    Escaping water through a crack makes a narrow high-frequency signature. The microphone rides past it, so the loudest sample marks the exact point rather than the nearest chamber.

  • Pressure as confirmation

    A leak pulls the line down locally. The pressure trace and the acoustic peak have to agree before the dashboard raises a repair order, which keeps false alarms off the crew's morning list.

  • Water Contact Sensor

    Tells the system when the ball is submerged, waking up the main electronics from sleep mode to conserve battery during transit.

  • DHT11 Sensor

    Monitors internal temperature and humidity to ensure the integrity of the titanium shell and prevent condensation damage to the electronics.

  • Sleeping to survive the run

    Out of the water it sleeps, so the cell is spent on the thirty kilometres that matter and the ball comes back with charge to spare.

Direct order, meter to confirm

The resident directly enters the required volume in the app to book water. The house flow sensor records what was actually delivered, so a short load is visible the same day instead of being argued about a month later.

Automated Complaints

If the ordered volume and the metered volume don't match, the app flags the discrepancy and lets the resident file a verified complaint in seconds.

Away mode watches the tank

Switch it on when the house is empty. Any sustained draw is unexpected by definition, and the alert arrives while there is still time to act.

One tap closes the main

The motorised valve sits on the house connection. Closing it from the phone stops a flooded floor, and reopening it is the same tap once the plumber has been.

Find it

The junction balance narrows the loss to a five kilometre zone within one billing cycle. The ball closes that to a metre in a single run.

Prove it

Acoustic peak, pressure drop and an unmapped IMU branch form the evidence pack that goes out with the crew, so the excavation happens once.

Keep it

Household meters and away mode stop the recovered water from leaking straight back out behind a wall.
